In English

Eipä Vissiin! is a one-day DIY festival in Oulu, Finland. Everything below in a nutshell:

  • Date: Saturday 12 September 2026
  • Doors: 15:00, first act 16:00, curfew 01:00
  • Venue: Tukikohta, Sorvarintie 5, 90520 Oulu
  • Age limit: 18
  • Tickets: 12€

Questions in English are very welcome: eipavissiinfestival@proton.me.
